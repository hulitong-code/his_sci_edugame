What do we call animals that eat only plants?
a. Herbivores
b. Carnivores
c. Omnivores
d. Decomposers
Explanation: Herbivores are animals that eat only plants, like cows, rabbits, and deer.

What do we call animals that eat both plants and other animals?
a. Omnivores
b. Carnivores
c. Herbivores
d. Producers
Explanation: Omnivores eat both plants and animals. Humans, bears, and pigs are examples of omnivores.

What is the process called when animals sleep through the winter?
a. Hibernation
b. Migration
c. Adaptation
d. Camouflage
Explanation: Hibernation is when animals enter a deep sleep during winter to conserve energy when food is scarce.

What do we call animals that have a backbone?
a. Vertebrates
b. Invertebrates
c. Mammals
d. Reptiles
Explanation: Vertebrates are animals with a backbone, including fish, birds, mammals, reptiles, and amphibians.

Which animal group gives birth to live young and feeds them milk?
a. Mammals
b. Birds
c. Reptiles
d. Amphibians
Explanation: Mammals give birth to live young and produce milk to feed their babies.

What do we call animals that eat only other animals?
a. Carnivores
b. Herbivores
c. Omnivores
d. Decomposers
Explanation: Carnivores are animals that eat only other animals, like lions, sharks, and eagles.

What is the process called when animals move to different places with the seasons?
a. Migration
b. Hibernation
c. Adaptation
d. Evolution
Explanation: Migration is when animals travel long distances to find food, better weather, or breeding grounds, often with the changing seasons.

What do we call animals without a backbone?
a. Invertebrates
b. Vertebrates
c. Mammals
d. Reptiles
Explanation: Invertebrates are animals without a backbone, including insects, spiders, worms, and jellyfish.

What is an adaptation?
a. A trait that helps an animal survive
b. When an animal moves
c. When an animal sleeps
d. When an animal eats
Explanation: An adaptation is a physical or behavioral trait that helps an animal survive in its environment, like camouflage or hibernation.

What do we call animals that are active at night?
a. Nocturnal
b. Diurnal
c. Hibernating
d. Migrating
Explanation: Nocturnal animals are active at night and sleep during the day, like owls, bats, and raccoons.

a. The path of energy from one living thing to another

c. Animals eating together
d. Plants growing in a line

Explanation: A food chain shows how energy flows from one organism to another, starting with plants (producers) and moving to animals (consumers).

What is camouflage?
a. When animals blend in with their surroundings
b. When animals are very colorful
c. When animals make sounds
d. When animals move fast
Image: camouflage.png
Explanation: Camouflage is when animals have colors or patterns that help them blend into their environment to hide from predators or prey.

What do we call the place where an animal lives?
a. Habitat
b. Home
c. Nest
d. Territory
Explanation: A habitat is the natural environment where an animal lives, including all the things it needs to survive like food, water, and shelter.

What is a predator?
a. An animal that hunts and eats other animals
b. An animal that is hunted
c. An animal that eats plants
d. An animal that lives in water
Explanation: A predator is an animal that hunts, kills, and eats other animals for food.

What is prey?
a. An animal that is hunted by predators
b. An animal that hunts others
c. An animal that eats plants
d. An animal that lives alone
Explanation: Prey are animals that are hunted and eaten by predators for food.

What is metamorphosis?
a. A dramatic change in an animal's body form
b. When an animal moves
c. When an animal sleeps
d. When an animal eats
Image: metamorphosis.png
Explanation: Metamorphosis is a major change in body form, like when a caterpillar becomes a butterfly or a tadpole becomes a frog.

What do we call animals that are warm-blooded?
a. Animals that maintain a constant body temperature
b. Animals that are always hot
c. Animals that change temperature
d. Animals that are cold
Explanation: Warm-blooded animals (like mammals and birds) maintain a constant body temperature regardless of the environment.

What is a life cycle?
a. The stages of an animal's life from birth to death
b. When an animal runs in circles
c. When an animal sleeps and wakes
d. When an animal eats and digests
Explanation: A life cycle shows all the stages an animal goes through during its lifetime, from birth or hatching to adulthood and reproduction.

What do we call animals that live in water and breathe with gills?
a. Fish
b. Mammals
c. Birds
d. Reptiles
Explanation: Fish are animals that live in water and use gills to extract oxygen from water, though some fish can also breathe air.
